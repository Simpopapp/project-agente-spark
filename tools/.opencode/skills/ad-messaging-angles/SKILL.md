# Source-backed angle bank

Extends `ad-research` when the task needs reusable messaging evidence. Paid ad campaign work only, never organic social posts or content outside an ad campaign. It does not invent testimonials, make unapproved claims, generate creative, or treat a source as proof of ad performance.

## Extraction

1. Use supplied reviews, interviews, support tickets, sales notes, site copy, and accessible public pages. Keep the URL or file source for every observation. When evidence is unavailable, request it instead of fabricating customer voice.
2. Cluster evidence into pains, desired outcomes, switching triggers, objections, identity, and proof. Preserve short exact phrases when they are useful and supported by their source.
3. For every angle: the audience and insight, a concise supported message, supporting sources, and a proof status: **strong** for repeated independent evidence, **moderate** for one specific source, **emerging** for a plausible but thin pattern.
4. Flag legal, substantiation, and brand risks. Do not turn a review, competitor claim, or search snippet into an approved product claim.

## The handoff

Rank up to three angles by relevance to the campaign goal and available proof; vary the proposition or proof, not just wording. Give `ad-research` the selected angles and their sources. In chat, state each selected angle as one plain sentence a non-marketer can judge, with no proof-status labels, clustering terms, or source mechanics.
